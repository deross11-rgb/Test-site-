Questions

Which watches work? +

Every Apple Watch from SE (2nd gen) and Series 6 up, and the app adapts honestly to your model. Garmin works too, via Apple Health, with reduced confidence honestly shown.

What does free include? +

Free forever: your readiness, sleep and load scores, workout logging, daily coach note, 7-day trends. Pro is the prescription engine: today's target, the modality selector, periodization, and the why.

Why no AI chat? +

Because a coach's value is the decision, not the conversation, the engine is the coach.

What happens to my data? +

Nowhere. Read from Apple Health with your permission, processed on your device, deleted when you delete the app.

Can I export it? +

Yes. Settings → Export, anytime. It's yours.
